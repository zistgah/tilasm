# CONTRACT overlay — zistgah/tilasm (tilasm)

Thin overlay on the master contract at `zistgah/governance`. It ADDS; it never relaxes.

1. **One engine.** `cycler.js` and `inbox.js` are shared, byte for byte, with every other cycler in
   this family. A cycler is a MODEL — steps, a context builder, a doctor, a payload shape — and
   nothing else may be forked into a variant.
2. **The deployment is kitab-shaped.** A work seeded here reads in the same reader as every other
   book in the estate.
3. **Endpoints are shapes, not providers.** The AI source table ships empty; the operator adds their
   own. At least one shipped path is local and keyless. No service is named anywhere.
4. **Filesystem visibility is not provenance.** The artefact inbox may say what is newly visible. It
   may never say who made it. An imported artefact records `operator-selected` and claims nobody.
5. **Nothing is hardcoded** — not a folder, not a provider, not a port assumption. A shipped default
   is fine; a shipped constant is not.
6. **Nothing pushes or mints without his typed gate.** An AI writes the script; he runs it.
7. **The tool's interface is read, never recalled** — and a discovered verb is not a discovered
   signature.
8. **The model's own refusals are contract, not style.** They are listed in CONTEXT.md and each one
   exists because publishing without it would be dishonest or unsafe. Removing one requires showing
   the failure can no longer occur.
